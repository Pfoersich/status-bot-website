Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

01

Information We Store

MC Status is designed to store only the information required for the bot to provide its functionality.

  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs
  • Discord user IDs for notification subscriptions
  • Minecraft server addresses configured for monitoring
02

How We Use Information

Stored information is used to monitor configured Minecraft servers, update Discord status messages and send requested notifications.

03

Information We Do Not Collect

MC Status does not intentionally collect private messages, message content, passwords, payment information or unrelated personal information.

04

Data Sharing

We do not sell or intentionally share collected information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

05

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the functionality of MC Status or until it is removed.

06

Data Deletion

If you want stored data associated with your Discord server or user ID to be deleted, contact the MC Status development team through the official support server.

07

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of MC Status changes or when required by applicable law.

08

Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact the MC Status development team through the official support server.
