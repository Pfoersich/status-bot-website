Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MC Status is designed to collect only the information required for the bot to provide its functionality.

  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role IDs used for notifications
  • Discord user IDs when users subscribe to notifications
  • Minecraft server addresses configured for monitoring

2. Why We Use This Information

The collected information is used exclusively to operate MC Status, including monitoring configured Minecraft servers, updating Discord messages, and sending requested notifications.

3. Data Storage

Configuration data may be stored by MC Status in order to maintain the bot's configuration between restarts.

MC Status does not intentionally collect private messages, message content, passwords, payment information, or other unrelated personal information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or intentionally share collected information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Configuration data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or until it is removed.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including requesting access to or deletion of stored information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of MC Status changes or when required by applicable law.

8. Contact

If you have questions about privacy or want to request deletion of data associated with MC Status, contact the development team through the official support channel.
